# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and technical interviewers who are scanning candidates on a laptop and need to understand Alfath's capabilities quickly. Their job is to see what he builds, how he approaches practical web problems, and whether there is enough signal to open a project or start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Alfatihnaman Badharija Putra Madvi as a fresh Informatics Engineering graduate and junior web developer through a concise, personal narrative. A dedicated portrait frame gives the introduction a human focal point while the copy stays honest about his early-career experience. Success means a visitor understands his profile and strongest work within 90 seconds, can inspect projects and credential evidence without friction, and can reach him from any device.

## Brand Personality

Bold, disciplined, and curious. The voice is direct and self-assured without overstating experience. The experience should feel energetic at the signature moments and quiet everywhere else.

## Anti-references

- Sci-fi dashboards, neon developer terminals, and generic SaaS interfaces.
- Paper, dossier, scrapbook, or faux-editorial portfolio treatments.
- Gradients, soft shadows, glassmorphism, and ornamental depth.
- Motion without narrative purpose, novelty cursors, or long horizontal-scroll showcases.

## Design Principles

- **Lead with proof.** Put the published ad-blocker research first, then support education and credential claims with viewable documents.
- **Make the scan effortless.** Each fold answers one question and keeps supporting copy concise.
- **Make scrolling tell the story.** Give every section one distinct, content-led scene while keeping Work as the only short pinned moment.
- **Use contrast as structure.** Flat color blocks, scale, and spacing establish depth without decoration.
- **Stay honest.** Make claims only where the portfolio has evidence and keep editable personal links centralized.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All interactive elements require visible keyboard focus, semantic labels, sufficient color contrast, and keyboard-operable project and document details. Credential PDFs must open from a details-first modal with a new-tab fallback. The authored full-motion experience is the fixed default and does not expose a motion preference control in the navigation. The project mosaic must preserve its reading order as it collapses from desktop grid to mobile list. The layout must remain usable from small mobile screens through large desktops.
